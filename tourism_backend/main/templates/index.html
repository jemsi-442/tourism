Popular Places to Explore

House of Wonders

A Stone Town landmark known for its bold architecture and rich island history.

Forodhani Gardens Night Market

An evening food market filled with seafood, street snacks, and oceanfront energy.

Stone Town

Walk winding streets, carved doors, and centuries of Swahili history.

Old Fort (Ngome Kongwe)

A historic fort that now hosts local events, crafts, and performances.

Spice Farms

Smell and taste the spices that helped make Zanzibar famous.

Nungwi Beach

Clear water, soft sand, and a lively beach atmosphere.

Kendwa Beach

A calm beach known for sunsets and relaxed seaside evenings.

Paje Beach

A long white-sand beach loved for kite surfing and laid-back coastal life.

Jozani Forest

A lush forest where you can spot the Zanzibar red colobus monkey.

Prison Island (Changuu)

Visit giant tortoises and enjoy a short boat trip from Stone Town.

Mnemba Atoll

A top spot for snorkeling, coral reefs, and bright turquoise water.

Kizimkazi Village

A coastal village known for dolphin tours and quiet ocean views.

The Rock Restaurant

A famous dining spot set on a rock above the Indian Ocean.

Dhow Sunset Cruise

Sail into sunset on a traditional wooden dhow with wide ocean views.
