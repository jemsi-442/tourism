Tanzania's Top National Parks

Serengeti National Park

Famous for the Great Migration, endless plains, and high density of predators.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Home to the world's largest volcanic caldera and the Big Five.

Kilimanjaro National Park

Features Africa's highest peak, Mount Kilimanjaro, a trekker's dream.

Tarangire National Park

Known for large herds of elephants and massive baobab trees.

Lake Manyara National Park

Diverse ecosystems, flamingos, and famous tree-climbing lions.

Selous Game Reserve

One of the largest faunal reserves, offering classic game drives and boat safaris.

Ruaha National Park

Rugged landscape, large elephant population, and rare predators.

Katavi National Park

Remote, untouched wilderness with massive herds of buffalo and hippos.

Mikumi National Park

Accessible from Dar es Salaam, excellent for seeing lions, giraffes, and elephants.

Kitulo National Park

The "Serengeti of Flowers"—a botanical marvel with orchids and wildflowers.
